# Agent Guidelines & Rules

## Communication Rules
- **Concise & Direct**: Always keep responses short, brief, and to the point. Avoid fluff or unnecessary explanations unless explicitly asked.
- **Flowcharts & Deep Explanations**: Place detailed explanations or architectural flowcharts in separate `.txt` files instead of chat responses.

## Technical Rules
- **Frontend Smoothness**: Prioritize UI smoothness and fast performance over heavy decoration.
- **Gitignore Maintenance**: Ensure `.gitignore` is kept updated with unnecessary files.
